Household Hazardous Waste Decision Router

Choose one of two supported jurisdictions and one of eight difficult household hazardous-waste cases. The router returns only what current official sources support.

Prototype scope: King County, Washington and Tempe, Arizona only. Eight item categories only. This is not emergency advice and does not replace local staff judgment.
Safety boundary: If a container is actively leaking, reacting, smoking, pressurized, or otherwise poses an immediate danger, do not rely on this prototype. Use local emergency or hazardous-material guidance.
