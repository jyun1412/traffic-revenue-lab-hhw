Privacy & Analytics

Last updated: 2026-09-17

This site is a bounded Traffic Revenue Lab field prototype. It provides source-linked household hazardous-waste routing information for King County, Washington and Tempe/Guadalupe, Arizona.

Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 only if you choose Allow analytics in the site banner. Until you consent, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded and this site sends no analytics data to Google.

If you consent, Google Analytics may collect standard web measurement data such as page views, session/referral information, device/browser information, and approximate geography as provided by Google Analytics. We also send two limited custom events:

  • router_result_rendered with the selected jurisdiction, item category, and result status.
  • official_source_clicked with the selected jurisdiction, item category, and result status.

We do not intentionally send your name, email address, street address, phone number, or other direct identifiers to Google Analytics. This prototype does not ask for a street address or free-text personal information.

Advertising features

This field probe does not use Google Ads remarketing, personalized advertising, or Google Signals. Advertising-related consent states are kept denied in the site implementation.

Your choice

You can use the router without analytics. Your preference is stored locally in your browser. You can reset the preference below and then return to the router to choose again.

Google processing

Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information as part of providing the hosting service under its own policies.

Experiment records

Traffic Revenue Lab may record aggregate experiment results such as total impressions, visits, router completions, and outbound official-source clicks in internal research records. These aggregate records are not intended to contain direct personal identifiers.
