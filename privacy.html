Privacy & Analytics

This is a bounded research prototype operated by Traffic Revenue Lab. It does not ask for names, email addresses, street addresses, phone numbers, or account creation.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is used only after you choose Allow analytics. Before consent, the Google Analytics script is not loaded by this site.

When enabled, analytics may collect standard technical and traffic information such as page views, session/referrer information, device/browser information, and approximate geography, plus two prototype-specific events:

  • router_result_rendered — includes the selected jurisdiction and item category.
  • official_source_clicked — includes the selected jurisdiction and item category.

We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, or other directly identifying form data to Google Analytics. The router has no free-text personal-data field.

Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choice

Your analytics choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember it. You can reset that choice below. Resetting also attempts to remove Google Analytics cookies accessible to this site.
