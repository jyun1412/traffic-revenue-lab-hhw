Privacy & Analytics

Last updated: 2026-09-20

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you choose Allow analytics. The experiment records page views, product selection/use, weather-result rendering, generic-vs-product comparison, official-source clicks, shares and data-insufficient outcomes.

We do not intentionally send your exact latitude/longitude, street address, raw weather payload, free-text notes, name or email as analytics event data.

Location

If you choose “Check my current NWS weather,” your browser asks for location permission. The coordinates are used in the browser to request an official National Weather Service point forecast. This prototype does not add those coordinates to Google Analytics events.

Hosting

This experiment is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its own policies.
